One place to share.

OneDrop moves text and files between your devices through your own OneDrive. No application server. No separate cloud to manage.

Learn how to installSee what it does
Small, focused, private

Everything you need for a handoff.

OneDrop is designed for moving your own work between devices, without turning that simple task into another social service.

Text and files

Send a note, a link, or an attachment from one OneDrop installation and pick it up on another.

OneDrive storage

Switch between up to three Microsoft accounts, each with its own messages and files in its OneDrive App Folder.

Recovery built in

Deleted messages can remain recoverable for the configured recovery period before physical cleanup.
