OneDrop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

OneDrop shares text and files between a user's own devices through the user's own OneDrive App Folder. OneDrop is provided without an application server or advertising service. It is available as Edge extensions and native Android and iOS apps.

Information OneDrop accesses

When you sign in, OneDrop receives the Microsoft account identity information needed to identify your session and obtains delegated access to the OneDrive App Folder belonging to that account. OneDrop reads and writes message documents, attachment metadata, and attachment files that you create or choose to synchronize. It does not read unrelated OneDrive files.

How information is used

Account information is used only to authenticate you and select your OneDrive App Folder. Messages and attachments are used only to synchronize the conversations and files you request. OneDrop uses Microsoft Graph over HTTPS and does not sell, rent, broker, or advertise against your content. OneDrop does not use your content for analytics or model training.

Local storage and downloads

Edge storage, IndexedDB, Cache Storage, and browser download records may contain preferences, synchronization metadata, temporary previews, and identifiers for files you downloaded. These records support the clients and are kept on your device. Native apps also use WebView local storage and app-owned download files. Exported copies are stored in the location you choose; opening or sharing a file gives the selected app access to that file. OneDrop does not automatically delete exported user-owned copies.

Your controls

You can sign out, revoke Microsoft consent in your Microsoft account settings, delete messages and attachments through OneDrop, and remove local extension data by removing the extension or clearing its site data. Native app data and private downloads can be removed through operating-system app controls. Sign out clears the locally stored authentication session. Revoking consent prevents future cloud access; existing files remain in your OneDrive until you delete them.

Service providers and security

Microsoft Entra ID and Microsoft Graph provide authentication and OneDrive access. OneDrop does not automatically send message or attachment content to other cloud services. Edge authentication tokens are stored in extension-local storage. Native iOS stores tokens in Keychain; native Android encrypts token state using an Android Keystore key.

Changes and contact
